About the Conference
Where Research Meets
the Road to Nusantara

NICE-EV 2026 is the premier international academic forum dedicated to Electric Vehicle architectures and their role in shaping the mobility infrastructure of Indonesia's new capital city, Nusantara (IKN).

Jointly organized by the Technical Hub Division of the IKN Authority (THD OIKN) and the Faculty of Science and Information Technology at the Institut Teknologi Kalimantan (FSTI ITK), this conference brings together researchers, engineers, policymakers, and industry leaders from across the globe.

From advanced power electronics to socio-economic policy, NICE-EV 2026 addresses the full spectrum of challenges and opportunities in building a smart, electric, and sustainable capital city from the ground up.

Research Scopes
Topics of Interest
01
Advanced Vehicle Propulsion & Power Electronics
Focuses on the internal architecture of the EV itself — from motor drives to thermal management in high-humidity tropical environments.
Motor Drives BMS Power Converters Fast-Charging Thermal Management
02
Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS) & V2X Connectivity
Digital architecture and vehicle-to-city communication. Targets computer science and telecommunications researchers.
V2X 5G Integration Autonomous Driving IoT Cybersecurity
03
Urban Mobility Architectures & Infrastructure Integration
Bridges civil engineering and urban design for EV-ready infrastructure in Nusantara's forest-city environment.
Wireless Charging Roads Modular Docking Mobility Hubs
04
Renewable Energy Integration & Charging Logistics
How EVs retrieve energy and manage demand within a smart city grid powered by renewables.
Solar Charging Energy Harvesting Battery Swapping Scheduling
05
Sustainable Materials & Circular Economy
Lifecycle analysis of EV technology — materials science and environmental engineering for mass EV adoption in Kalimantan.
Battery Recycling Second-Life LCA Lightweight Materials
06
Policy, Socio-Economics & Human-Centric Mobility
The "social architecture" — how electric mobility transition affects people and the economy of Nusantara.
Techno-Economic Analysis Regulatory Frameworks Consumer Behavior Inclusive Design
